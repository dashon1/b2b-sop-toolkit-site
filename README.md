# B2B Compliance-Ready SOP Toolkit

**Digital product for B2B businesses in regulated industries — $67 Essential / $147 Complete**

A done-for-you Standard Operating Procedure system built for healthcare (HIPAA), finance (SOC 2), and legal/regulated industries. Get audit-ready with professional SOPs that satisfy compliance reviewers and internal audits without hiring a consultant.

---

## The Problem

B2B businesses in regulated industries face the same problem: they know they need SOPs, but building them from scratch takes weeks of time they don't have. Consultants charge $5,000–$20,000 for what amounts to customized templates. Meanwhile, auditors are getting sharper, and "we're working on it" no longer satisfies them.

---

## What's Inside

### Essential — $67

- **Core SOP Templates** (15+ templates) — Employee onboarding, data handling, incident response, vendor management, and more
- **Compliance Mapping Matrix** — Maps each SOP to its applicable regulation (HIPAA, SOC 2, ISO 27001)
- **SOP Implementation Checklist** — Step-by-step rollout guide for your team
- **Policy Acknowledgment Tracker** — Document that employees have read and acknowledged each policy

### Complete — $147

Everything in Essential, plus:

- **Industry-Specific SOP Packs** — Pre-built SOPs for healthcare, finance, and legal (3 separate packs)
- **Audit Preparation Guide** — How to organize, present, and defend your SOPs to an auditor
- **SOP Review & Version Control System** — Keep your SOPs current and auditable over time
- **90-Day Implementation Roadmap** — Full rollout plan with milestones and accountability checkpoints
